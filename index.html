WHY THIS EXISTS

The usual mess: shapefiles in email, a geodatabase nobody else can open, or a web map with no file you can take home.

Vector Ledger: a hosted editor with an approval trail and GeoParquet / PMTiles you can download.

Data platforms like CARTO and Wherobots are strong at lakehouse analytics. We are aimed at the people who still have to change the lines on the map.
